About Me

Life Before School

I grew up in Worcester, Pennsylvania, where I spent most of my childhood immersed in video games. From a young age, I was captivated by the worlds inside those games — not just playing them, but wondering how they were made. What made consoles run? How powerful were they behind the scenes? And more importantly: what was this thing called “code” that made everything work?

Technology quickly became more than a hobby — it became an escape and a passion. It gave me comfort, distraction, and inspiration all at once. I knew early on that I wanted to explore how the tech I loved actually functioned.

Education

I attended Methacton High School and graduated in 2018. After taking time to reflect and explore my interests, I enrolled at Temple University in 2021 and graduated in 2025 with a degree in Information Science & Technology.

At Temple, I built a strong foundation in software development, databases, systems analysis, and web technologies — all while discovering how much I enjoyed creating full-scale applications that work across the stack.

Why Full Stack Development?

Building this portfolio using React and Next.js has been an incredibly rewarding experience. Full stack development gives me the ability to bring an idea to life from both the front-end and the back-end — crafting both the interface you see and the logic that powers it.

It’s the perfect combination of creativity and engineering, and I love the challenge of constantly learning and solving problems along the way.

I hope you enjoy exploring this site as much as I’ve enjoyed building it!
